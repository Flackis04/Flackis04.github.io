Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This app is a personal tool used only by its owner to create videos and publish them to the owner's own TikTok account.

What data the app uses

  • Basic TikTok account information (username, display name, avatar) to show which account a video will be posted to.
  • An access token from TikTok, used only to upload videos the owner has approved.

How data is stored

Tokens and videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

You can revoke this app's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
