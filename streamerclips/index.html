Good to know

Is clipping other streamers allowed?

Only with permission. StreamerClips is for streamers clipping their own streams, and for clippers in a streamer's official clipping program. You're responsible for having the rights to what you post.

Does it post publicly on its own?

No. You download your clips and post them yourself, so nothing goes out without you seeing it first.

Which platforms and games?

Twitch, Kick and YouTube recordings in (and Twitch clip links), TikTok and YouTube Shorts formats out. It's tuned for GTA 6, but works with any game.

How do credits work?

A 6-hour stream is 6 credits, whether it gives you one banger or five. Scan just part of a stream to spend less. Failed runs are refunded automatically.
