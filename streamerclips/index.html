How it works

Paste a VOD link once, or let the autopilot check your streamers every morning.

01 · FIND

Real moments, not guesses

Reads which moments viewers clipped on Twitch and how chat reacted, so it starts where the audience already reacted.

02 · PICK

AI that watches the video

Claude reads the transcript and looks at the frames, scores each moment honestly and drops anything that needs context.

03 · EDIT

Built for retention

Facecam on top, gameplay below, word-by-word captions, a hook card, dead air cut out, and a punch-in on the peak.

04 · POST

Straight to TikTok

Every clip lands in your TikTok inbox, ready to post. A 15–40 s Shorts cut and a 60 s+ TikTok cut of each moment.

Questions

Is clipping other streamers allowed?

Only with permission. StreamerClips is for streamers clipping their own streams, and for clippers working in a streamer's official clipping program. You're responsible for having the rights to the content you post.

Does it post publicly on its own?

Clips go to your TikTok inbox as drafts. You add a sound and tap Post, so nothing goes out without you seeing it first.

Which platforms and games?

Twitch VODs in, TikTok and YouTube Shorts formats out. It's tuned for GTA (including GTA 6), but works with any game.
